## Full description

WIMAX is a voice-first productivity workspace that turns unstructured speech into useful, connected work.

Capture ideas, reflections, habit updates, tasks and financial notes without choosing a category first. WIMAX transcribes the recording, removes verbal clutter and prepares structured results for review.

Use Meeting Mode for longer sessions with participant-notice confirmation, resilient upload, speaker-aware transcription, chapters, decisions and action items.

Build and edit pages, databases, projects and tasks in one workspace. Search connected knowledge in natural language and return to the original context when you need it.

Update habits by speaking naturally, follow routines and energy patterns, and receive concise weekly briefings and coaching prompts.

Voice commands can navigate the app, create pages and tables, update records and prepare supported actions. Sensitive external actions require confirmation.

Privacy controls are built in: mobile credentials stay in protected device storage, structured data can be exported, and the account and associated active-service data can be permanently deleted from Settings.

WIMAX does not sell private voice content or use it for advertising. Microphone access is used only when you start Voice Capture or Meeting Mode. Recording laws vary; always notify participants and obtain any required consent or other lawful basis.
