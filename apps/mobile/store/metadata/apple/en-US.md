## Description

WIMAX is a voice-first productivity workspace. Capture an unstructured thought and turn it into useful work without stopping to organise it by hand.

VOICE CAPTURE

Record an idea, reflection, habit update, task or financial note. WIMAX transcribes it, removes verbal clutter and structures the result for review.

MEETING MODE

Record longer sessions with participant-consent confirmation, resumable upload, speaker-aware transcription, chapters, decisions and action items.

AN ADAPTABLE WORKSPACE

Keep pages, databases, projects and tasks together. Search your connected knowledge in natural language and return to the original context when needed.

HABITS AND PATTERNS

Update habits by speaking naturally. See routines, energy signals and progress over time, then receive concise weekly briefings and coaching prompts.

VOICE COMMANDS

Navigate WIMAX, create pages and tables, update records and prepare supported external actions by voice. Sensitive actions require confirmation.

PRIVACY CONTROLS

Your mobile sign-in token is protected by Keychain or Android Keystore. Export your structured workspace or permanently delete your account from Settings. WIMAX does not sell private voice content or use it for advertising.

Microphone access is used only for voice capture and Meeting Mode. Meeting recording laws vary; always notify participants and obtain any consent or lawful basis required where you are.

## App Review notes

WIMAX requires an account because captures and workspace records synchronize between web and mobile. A review account must be supplied in App Review Information immediately before submission. Microphone permission is requested to demonstrate Voice Capture. Meeting Mode always shows a participant-notice confirmation before recording. Account deletion is available from the Settings icon in the app. Privacy, Terms and deletion information are also accessible from Settings.
