# WIMAX age and content rating answers — release 1.0.0

## Product audience

- Intended audience: **16+ / adults and working-age users**
- Not designed for children
- Do not select any Google target age group under 16
- No ads, gambling, contests, dating, public chat, public UGC feed or in-app purchases in release 1.0.0

## Apple age-rating questionnaire draft

Answer **None / No** for:

- Cartoon/fantasy, realistic or prolonged graphic violence
- Profanity or crude humor supplied by the app
- Horror/fear themes
- Medical/treatment information
- Alcohol, tobacco or drug references supplied by the app
- Sexual content or nudity
- Gambling, simulated gambling, contests or loot boxes
- Unrestricted web access
- Public messaging/chat and public user-generated content
- Advertising

User voice notes are private account content, not published or discovered by other users. Complete the live questionnaire truthfully; Apple calculates the final regional rating.

## Google Play content rating draft

- Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other non-game application** (choose the closest option shown)
- Violence: No
- Sexuality/nudity: No
- Language: No app-provided offensive language
- Controlled substances: No
- Gambling: No
- User interaction/public sharing: No
- Online content: Account-synchronized private content only
- Location sharing: No
- Digital purchases: No

Expected rating is suitable for a broad audience, but WIMAX should still be targeted at **16+** because it processes personal voice, meeting and financial-note content. The store's rating authority, not this draft, determines the displayed rating.
